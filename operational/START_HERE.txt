RECRUITING INTAKE SYSTEM — START HERE
====================================

1. EXTRACT all files and folders from this ZIP. Do not launch HTML directly
   from inside the ZIP. The two pages need their assets/ folders.

2. Open Recruiting_Intake_Kiosk.html in desktop Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
   Click Select lead file, then choose the included Recruiting_Leads.csv.
   Use fictional data for the first test.

3. After intake is finished, CLOSE the kiosk. Open
   Recruiting_Staff_Manager.html, select the SAME CSV, then review/copy/QR
   the FirstName|PhoneNumber list for your existing iPhone Shortcut.

4. TO EDIT WORDING, COLORS, OR QR BATCH SIZE, open:
       assets/js/Recruiting_Config.js
   in VS Code or Notepad++. Save, then refresh the pages.

5. FOR FULL DOCUMENTATION, READ:
       README.md
       docs/CUSTOMIZATION_GUIDE.md
       docs/TECHNICAL_REFERENCE.md
       docs/TESTING_CHECKLIST.md

WARNING: The local CSV is unencrypted and these pages have no login. This is
not an officially approved Air Force information system. Use real student
information only after the recruiting organization authorizes the devices,
privacy notice, consent process and data transfer. Do not send lead data
through personal email.


Background image note:
- The student page background image is stored at assets/images/Kiosk_Background.jpg
- Replace that file if you want a different image later.

V5 LOOK AND FEEL: Both screens use a coordinated Air Force-inspired visual theme.
Brand words/colors: assets/js/Recruiting_Config.js
Design styling: assets/css/Recruiting_Kiosk.css and Recruiting_Manager.css


VERSION 6 CONTACT TRACKING:
- After your iPhone sends a QR batch, return to the PC.
- In the QR dialog, uncheck any messages that did not send.
- Click Confirm sent / mark contacted to save their statuses immediately.
- To remove a lead, select its checkbox in the staff table and click Delete selected.
- Scanning alone does NOT synchronize the two devices; staff must confirm successful sends.
