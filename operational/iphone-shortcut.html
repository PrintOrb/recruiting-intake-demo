After installation

  1. Open the Recruiting Text Assistant in the Shortcuts app and review its actions and message template.
  2. Confirm the shortcut includes Scan QR or Barcode → Copy to Clipboard → Get Clipboard, before the name and phone processing loop.
  3. In the recruiter dashboard, select Show QR for iPhone for an eligible lead batch, then run the Shortcut to scan it.
  4. Review each intended recipient and message before sending; afterward, confirm only successfully sent recipients on the dashboard.
